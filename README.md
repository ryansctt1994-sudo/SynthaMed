# SynthaMed

**Control is not authority.**

SynthaMed is a healthcare intelligence architecture for patient evidence sovereignty, clinician-governed review, and prevention of institutional or AI authority overreach.

It is organized into three core layers:

- **SynthaMed** — the public healthcare platform.
- **Chaos Command** — the patient-side evidence custody and sovereignty engine.
- **CATHEDRAL-OS** — the clinician-side CDS governance and safety architecture.

The system is designed to preserve lived health evidence, surface clinically relevant context, and support clinician review without allowing AI outputs, administrative processes, institutional pressure, or infrastructure control to silently become medical authority.

> The pattern is not the person.  
> The model is not the clinician.  
> The institution is not the owner of the patient record.

## Architecture Layers

### SynthaMed

The public healthcare platform layer. SynthaMed packages the architecture into workflows for patients, clinicians, and institutions.

### Chaos Command

The patient-side evidence sovereignty engine. It preserves lived events, symptoms, contradictions, functional decline, uncertainty, consent receipts, and bounded evidence exports.

Chaos Command does **not** diagnose, prescribe, or override clinicians.

### CATHEDRAL-OS

The clinician-side decision-support governance engine. It provides evidence trace, safety review, authority routing, audit receipts, and non-autonomous clinical decision support.

CATHEDRAL-OS does **not** replace clinician judgment or issue autonomous medical orders.
