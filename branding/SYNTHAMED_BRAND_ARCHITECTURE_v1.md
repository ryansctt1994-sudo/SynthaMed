## 2. Naming Logic

The naming stack works because each name carries a different burden.

### SynthaMed

Question answered: What is this?

SynthaMed is the public-facing product layer. It sounds like a healthcare platform, can sit in front of clinicians and institutions, and avoids the friction created by more aggressive internal architecture names.

Public meaning:

**SynthaMed delivers evidence-informed healthcare workflows.**

### Chaos Command

Question answered: Why does this exist?

Chaos Command preserves the original philosophical insight: human reality is messy, contradictory, temporal, incomplete, and vulnerable to institutional compression.

It should not be the primary healthcare-facing brand, because “chaos” can imply instability in clinical contexts. But as an internal architecture name, it is strong.

Architectural meaning:

**Chaos Command preserves reality before interpretation.**

### CATHEDRAL-OS

Question answered: How is clinical authority governed?

CATHEDRAL-OS carries the weight of clinical seriousness, safety, validation, and authority-routing. It is the place where evidence becomes clinician-reviewable reasoning, but never autonomous clinical command.

Clinical meaning:

**CATHEDRAL-OS governs interpretation before action.**

No layer may silently inherit authority from another layer.

## 4. Canonical Layer Definitions

### Platform Layer — SynthaMed

SynthaMed is the product and user-facing healthcare platform. It packages the architecture into usable workflows for patients, clinicians, and institutions.

It may include:

- patient app;
- clinician review interface;
- consented evidence exports;
- CDS review panels;
- safety summaries;
- audit receipts;
- longitudinal evidence timelines.

SynthaMed must not claim autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, triage disposition, or treatment authority.

### Patient Evidence Layer — Chaos Command

Chaos Command is the patient-side evidence sovereignty engine.

It preserves:

- raw events;
- symptoms;
- context;
- contradictions;
- functional decline;
- uncertainty;
- epoch history;
- consent receipts;
- personal annotations;
- export boundaries.

Its root invariant:

**No health pattern may silently convert into diagnosis, identity, treatment advice, or authority.**

### Clinical Governance Layer — CATHEDRAL-OS

CATHEDRAL-OS is the clinician-side CDS governance engine.

It governs:

- evidence normalization;
- clinical state computation;
- safety gates;
- authority routing;
- CDS output;
- clinician override;
- validation;
- audit trace.

Its root invariant:

**Medical model output is not clinical authority.**

### Governance Layer

The governance layer defines capture and conversion risks.

Core files:

## 5. Unified Doctrine

The whole healthcare branch now reduces to one principle:

**Control is not authority.**

This single sentence unifies all major artifacts.

It means:

- controlling the insurance system does not create authority over medical truth;
- controlling the hospital does not create authority over patient identity;
- controlling the algorithm does not create authority over treatment;
- controlling the workflow does not create authority over clinician judgment;
- controlling data storage does not create ownership of the patient record;
- controlling access does not create epistemic legitimacy.

The three doctrine lines should remain attached to every major handoff:

**The pattern is not the person.**  
**The model is not the clinician.**  
**The institution is not the owner of the patient record.**

Together:

**Control is not authority. Evidence survives interpretation. Human authority remains reviewable, accountable, and consent-bound.**
